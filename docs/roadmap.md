# Roadmap

The first prototype is one owner, one agent and one Aevum vault, with versioned mandates and independently enforced spending limits.

Before submission: finish live credentials/configuration, deploy the GenLayer contract and Aevum vault, capture actual approval/payment references, verify hosted recovery, publish the working deployment and accessible video, and confirm team registration.

After testnet validation: user interviews and paid-service experiments; policy simulation before activation; multi-agent budgets and delegated financial authority; verified merchant evidence; scalable per-request persistence; monitoring/key-management improvements; independently verifiable cross-chain authorization research; security review and operational readiness before any mainnet deployment.

Account abstraction, gas sponsorship, bridges, streaming payments, and broad marketplace protocols are deferred until supported infrastructure and a concrete user need justify them.
